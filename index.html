What's the difference between single-vendor and multi-vendor e-commerce apps?

A single-vendor app is a store where one owner sells their products to many customers. A multi-vendor app is a marketplace where many sellers sell their products to many customers.

How much does it cost to develop an e-commerce app?

Developing an e-commerce app from scratch typically costs between ₹80,000 and ₹20 lakhs in India. With 10xCart, you pay a one-time setup cost and a monthly maintenance fee — a much more competitive price, without compromising on quality.

Why is there a one-time setup cost?

Setting up a shop and mobile apps isn't something a non-technical owner should have to do alone. Our development team handles the complete setup for you, which is covered by the one-time cost.

What does the monthly maintenance fee cover?

It covers ongoing maintenance, updates, bug fixes and support for your store and apps, billed monthly. You provide your own domain and cloud server, so you stay in full control of your hosting.

Do I need my own domain and server?

Yes. You buy and own the domain name and the cloud server (hosting). We set up your store and apps on it, connect your domain and keep everything maintained. Not sure what to buy? Ask us and we'll suggest options that fit your store.

Which platforms do you develop for — iOS, Android or both?

Both. We develop for iOS and Android, making sure each app is built to an exceptional standard — and we can launch your website too.

Can you customise the app design to match our brand?

Yes. We customise your store and app design — colours, logo, layout and more — to match your brand.

What features are essential for an e-commerce app?

Every successful e-commerce store should have:

  • Responsive, good-looking design
  • Fast loading speed
  • Easy navigation and site search
  • A smooth cart and secure payments

All of these come built in with 10xCart.

Are admin panels provided to manage products, orders and vendors?

Yes. You get admin panels to easily manage products, orders and vendors — plus a dedicated vendor app on multi-vendor plans.

Can the app be integrated with our existing website or systems?

10xCart is a complete 360-degree solution — storefront, apps, admin and payments — so most stores don't need any other system. If your business needs something beyond that, talk to our custom software team.

Do you offer maintenance and support after launch?

Yes. Ongoing maintenance and support are included in your monthly maintenance plan once your store goes live.

Do you build SaaS products or custom software too?

Yes. Beyond e-commerce, our team builds SaaS products, web apps, mobile apps and custom business software. Share your idea through the form below and we'll get back to you.
